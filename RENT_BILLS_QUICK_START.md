# Rent & Bills - Quick Start Guide

## For Managers 👨‍💼

### Step 1: Add Rent-Only Members

1. **Navigate to Rent & Bills**
   - Click "Rent & Bills" in the left sidebar

2. **Go to Members Tab**
   - Click the "Members" tab at the top

3. **Add a New Member**
   - Click "Add Member" button
   - Fill in the form:
     - **Email**: The member's Gmail address (required)
     - **Full Name**: Their full name (optional, defaults to email)
     - **Nickname**: A friendly nickname (optional)
   - Click "Add Member"

4. **Repeat** for all rent-only members

💡 **Tip**: Rent-only members can login with their Gmail and will only see their bills!

---

### Step 2: Create Bills

1. **Go to Bills Tab**
   - Click the "Bills" tab

2. **Click the Floating "+" Button**
   - Located at the bottom-right corner

3. **Fill in the Bill Details**
   - **Bill Type**: Choose from:
     - Rent
     - Electricity Bill
     - Water Bill
     - Gas Bill
     - Internet Bill
     - Other
   
   - **Member**: Select who this bill is for
     - Choose from household members
     - Or choose from rent-only members
   
   - **Amount**: Enter the bill amount (৳)
   
   - **Due Date**: When the bill is due
   
   - **Status**: 
     - Unpaid (default)
     - Partial (if partially paid)
     - Paid (if fully paid)
   
   - **Description**: Brief description (optional)
   
   - **Notes**: Additional notes (optional)

4. **Click "Create Bill"**

---

### Step 3: Record Payments

When a member pays rent or a bill:

1. **Find the Bill**
   - Use filters if needed (status/type)

2. **Click "Payment" Button**
   - On the bill card

3. **Enter Payment Details**
   - **Amount**: How much was paid
   - Use quick buttons:
     - **Half**: Pay half the remaining amount
     - **Full**: Pay the full remaining amount
   - **Payment Notes**: Optional notes

4. **Click "Record Payment"**

✅ The bill status will automatically update:
- Unpaid → Partial (if partially paid)
- Unpaid/Partial → Paid (if fully paid)

---

### Step 4: Manage Bills

#### Edit a Bill
1. Click "Edit" on any bill card
2. Update the details
3. Click "Update Bill"

#### Delete a Bill
1. Click "Delete" on any bill card
2. Confirm deletion

## For Regular Members 👥

### Viewing Rent & Bills

1. **Navigate to Rent & Bills**
   - Click "Rent & Bills" in the sidebar

2. **View All Bills**
   - See all rent and bills for all members
   - Use filters to find specific bills:
     - Filter by status (unpaid/partial/paid)
     - Filter by type (rent/electricity/etc.)

3. **Check Dashboard**
   - The main dashboard shows a Rent & Bills summary
   - View recent bills
   - See overdue alerts

⚠️ **Note**: Regular members can only VIEW bills, not create or edit them.

---

## For Rent-Only Members 🏠

### Your Limited Access

When you login, you will:

1. **See Only Your Bills**
   - Your assigned rent and bills
   - Payment status
   - Due dates

2. **Dashboard Summary**
   - Quick overview of your bills
   - Overdue alerts

3. **Restrictions**
   - ❌ Cannot create or edit bills
   - ❌ Cannot record payments
   - ❌ Cannot access household expenses
   - ❌ Cannot access other features

💡 **Contact Your Manager** if you need any changes to your bills.

## Common Questions

### Q: Can I add the same person as both a household member and rent-only member?
**A:** No need! If they're already a household member, just assign bills to them directly.

### Q: What happens if I delete a rent-only member who has bills?
**A:** The bills remain, but you'll see a warning. Bills are not automatically deleted.

### Q: Can I edit a paid bill?
**A:** Yes, managers can edit any bill at any time.

### Q: How do rent-only members login?
**A:** They use the same Google Sign-In as everyone else, but only see their bills.

### Q: Can I export bill data?
**A:** Currently, you can view in the dashboard. Export features may be added in the future.

### Q: What if I make a payment mistake?
**A:** Managers can edit the bill to correct the paid amount.

## Tips & Tricks

### Quick Payment Recording
1. Use the "Full" button to instantly pay the entire remaining amount
2. Use the "Half" button for split payments

### Organizing Bills
1. Use consistent naming in descriptions
2. Set due dates at the start of the month for monthly bills
3. Use the "Other" category for one-time expenses

### Staying on Top of Payments
1. Check the dashboard daily for overdue alerts
2. Filter by "Unpaid" to see what needs attention
3. Use the "Partial" filter to follow up on incomplete payments
