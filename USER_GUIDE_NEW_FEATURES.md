## 1. Category-Based Expenses

### What is it?
Organize your expenses into categories like Groceries, Dining, Utilities, Rent, etc.

### How to use:
1. When adding a new expense, you'll see a grid of category icons
2. Click on the category that best matches your expense (e.g., 🛒 Groceries)
3. The category will be saved with your expense
4. View category breakdowns in Analytics and Reports

### Benefits:
- Understand where your money goes
- Filter expenses by category
- Set category-specific budgets
- Better financial insights

## 2. Budget Management

### What is it?
Set spending limits and track how much you've spent against your budget.

### How to use:

#### Creating a Budget:
1. Click "Budget" in the sidebar
2. Click "+ Add Budget"
3. Fill in:
   - **Budget Name**: e.g., "Monthly Groceries"
   - **Budget Type**: 
     - *Household*: For total household spending
     - *Category*: For specific categories (Groceries, Utilities, etc.)
     - *Member*: For individual member spending
   - **Budget Limit**: Amount in ৳
   - **Period**: Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly
4. Click "Create Budget"

#### Tracking Your Budget:
- View real-time progress bars
- Green = Under 80% spent (Good!)
- Yellow = 80-100% spent (Warning)
- Red = Over budget (Exceeded)
- See exact amounts spent and remaining

#### Budget Recommendations:
- System analyzes your last 3 months of spending
- Suggests recommended budget amounts
- Helps you set realistic limits

### Tips:
- Start with a household budget to track total spending
- Add category budgets for better control
- Set member budgets to ensure fair distribution
- Check budget page regularly to stay on track

## 3. Recurring Expenses

### What is it?
Automate regular expenses that repeat on a schedule (rent, utilities, subscriptions).

### How to use:

#### Creating a Recurring Expense:
1. Click "Recurring" in the sidebar
2. Click "+ Add Recurring Expense"
3. Fill in:
   - **Name**: e.g., "Monthly House Rent"
   - **Category**: Select appropriate category
   - **Amount**: Fixed amount in ৳
   - **Frequency**: Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly
   - **Buyer**: Who pays it
   - **Shared Among**: Who shares the cost
   - **Start Date**: When to start creating expenses
   - **Auto-approve**: Check if this expense should auto-approve (for trusted recurring costs)
4. Click "Create"

#### Managing Recurring Expenses:
- **Pause**: Temporarily stop creation (useful for seasonal expenses)
- **Resume**: Restart a paused recurring expense
- **Edit**: Update amount, frequency, or other details
- **Delete**: Permanently remove the template

#### How it works:
- System checks every hour for due recurring expenses
- Automatically creates expenses on schedule
- Tracks how many times each template has been used
- Shows next occurrence date

### Best for:
- Monthly rent
- Utility bills (electricity, water, internet)
- Subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, etc.)
- Weekly groceries
- Any predictable, regular expense

## 4. Savings Goals

### What is it?
Set financial goals and track progress towards them.

### How to use:

#### Creating a Goal:
1. Click "Savings Goals" in the sidebar
2. Click "+ Add Goal"
3. Fill in:
   - **Goal Name**: e.g., "Emergency Fund"
   - **Description**: Optional details
   - **Target Amount**: How much you want to save
   - **Current Amount**: How much you've already saved
   - **Deadline**: When you want to achieve the goal
4. Click "Create Goal"

#### Adding Contributions:
- Use quick buttons: +৳100, +৳500, +৳1000
- Or click "Edit" to enter custom amounts

#### Tracking Progress:
- Visual progress bars show percentage complete
- Days remaining countdown
- Color changes when goal is achieved (green checkmark)
- Overdue goals show in red

### Ideas for Goals:
- Emergency fund (3-6 months expenses)
- Vacation fund
- New appliance or furniture
- Holiday shopping budget
- Home repairs reserve

## 5. Inventory Management

### What is it?
Track household items, get low stock alerts, and generate shopping lists.

### How to use:

#### Adding Items:
1. Click "Inventory" in the sidebar
2. Click "+ Add Item"
3. Fill in:
   - **Item Name**: e.g., "Rice"
   - **Category**: Pantry, Refrigerated, Frozen, etc.
   - **Current Quantity**: How much you have
   - **Min Quantity**: Alert when stock falls below this
   - **Unit**: kg, L, pieces, pack, bottle, etc.
   - **Avg. Price**: Optional, for value tracking
   - **Expiry Date**: Optional, for perishables
4. Click "Add Item"

#### Managing Stock:
- Use **-1, +1, +5** buttons for quick updates
- Edit items to adjust quantities manually
- Delete items no longer needed

#### Shopping List:
- Click "Shopping List" button
- See all low/out-of-stock items
- View estimated costs
- Items marked as High/Medium priority

#### Dashboard:
- Total items count
- Low stock alerts (orange)
- Out of stock alerts (red)
- Total inventory value

### Tips:
- Add items as you buy them
- Update quantities after cooking
- Set realistic min quantities
- Check before grocery shopping

## 6. Debt Settlement

### What is it?
Easy way to record debt payments and track outstanding balances.

### How to use:

#### Making a Payment:
1. Go to "Debts" page
2. Find the debt you want to pay
3. Choose payment method:
   - **Settle Full Amount**: Pay entire remaining balance
   - **Quick Pay** (৳100, ৳500): Pay a fixed amount
   - **Custom**: Enter any amount
4. Add payment note (optional): e.g., "Bank transfer"
5. Click "Record Payment"

#### Tracking Payments:
- Progress bar shows how much has been paid
- See original amount vs remaining amount
- Last payment date displayed
- Full payment history available

#### Status Indicators:
- **Active**: Debt still has remaining balance
- **Paid**: Fully settled debt (🎉)
- **Pending**: Awaiting manager approval

### For Debtors (People who owe):
- See "I Owe" summary card (red)
- Click to pay debts quickly
- Reduce your balance

### For Creditors (People owed money):
- See "Owed to Me" summary card (green)
- Track when others pay you
- Mark payments as received

## 7. Analytics & Trends

### What is it?
Visual insights into your spending patterns with charts and statistics.

### How to use:

#### Accessing Analytics:
1. Click "Analytics" in the sidebar
2. Select time range:
   - Last 7 Days
   - Last 30 Days
   - Last 3 Months
   - Last Year

#### Understanding the Dashboard:

**Summary Cards:**
- **Total Spending**: Total approved expenses
- **Average**: Average per expense
- **Transactions**: Number of expenses
- **Trend**: % change from previous period

**Charts:**
1. **Spending by Category** (Pie Chart):
   - See which categories consume most money
   - Click sections for details

2. **Spending by Member** (Bar Chart):
   - Compare spending across household members
   - Identify high spenders

3. **Spending Over Time** (Line Chart):
   - Track spending trends
   - Identify spending spikes

4. **Top Categories** (Progress Bars):
   - Ranked list of highest-spending categories
   - Percentage of total spending

### What to Look For:
- Unexpected spending spikes
- High-cost categories that can be reduced
- Unfair distribution among members
- Seasonal patterns (higher spending in certain months)

## 10. Receipt Upload

### What is it?
Attach photo receipts to expenses for proof and record-keeping.

### How to use:

#### Adding Receipts:
1. While adding/editing expense
2. Scroll to "Receipt Images" section
3. Click "Upload Receipts (Max 5)"
4. Select images from device
   - Can select multiple at once
   - Supports JPG, PNG, etc.
5. Preview appears below
6. Click X to remove unwanted receipts
7. Submit expense with receipts

#### Viewing Receipts:
1. Open expense details
2. Scroll to "Receipt Images" section
3. See thumbnail previews
4. Click any receipt to view full size
5. Click outside to close

### Tips:
- Take clear, well-lit photos
- Include entire receipt
- Max 5 receipts per expense
- Helps with disputes and audits

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Questions:

**Q: Can I edit a budget after creating it?**
A: Yes! Click the "Edit" button on any budget card.

**Q: What happens if I pause a recurring expense?**
A: It stops creating new expenses until you resume it.

**Q: Can members see all budgets?**
A: Members can see budgets they're part of. Managers see all budgets.

**Q: How do I know if I'm over budget?**
A: Budget cards turn red when exceeded, and you'll get a notification.

**Q: Can I have multiple savings goals?**
A: Yes! Create as many goals as you need.

**Q: What if inventory item has no expiry date?**
A: Leave it blank. Expiry dates are optional.

**Q: Do debt payments need approval?**
A: No, debt payments are recorded immediately.

**Q: Can I export analytics data?**
A: Yes, use the Reports page to export to Excel or CSV.
